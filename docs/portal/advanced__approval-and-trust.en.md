# Approvals, trust and management

- **Waiting**: the risky operations a controller has sent, one by one — allow
  once, remember, or refuse.
- **Trusted controllers**: who can drive this device, at a glance, and you can
  **revoke** any of them at any time.
- **Mode**: Ask me (anything no rule covers comes to you) or Allow all
  (everything runs unattended, for trusted devices only).
- **Rules**: pre-authorize the safe commands you use often, so you stop
  clicking.
- **Remove this device**: top right of the device page, takes it out of your
  namespace.
- **Shared devices**: hand another user rights on one of your devices — you
  have to be friends first, see "Invites, friends and sharing".
