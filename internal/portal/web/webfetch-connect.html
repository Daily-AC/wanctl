Send one line to an AI chat that can read URLs. It reads the rest from wanctl and tells you when to approve.

中文

请打开 {{.Relay}}/webfetch 并按它说的做，帮我操作我的设备。

English

Open {{.Relay}}/webfetch and follow what it says, so you can operate my device for me.

What happens next

  1. The AI opens the link and asks wanctl for access to your devices.
  2. It gives you an approval link. You pick the devices and how long, and approve.
  3. On its first command you approve pairing once. After that it just works.

Nothing is authorized by opening or copying this page. Device rules, the approved duration and revocation all still apply, and you can share this line with anyone who has their own wanctl account.
